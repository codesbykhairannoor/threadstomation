Threadstomation Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Threadstomation. We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our social media automation web application.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Threadstomation and connect your third-party social media accounts (such as TikTok or Threads), we collect the following information:

  • Authentication Data: OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens necessary to authenticate API requests on your behalf.
  • Basic Profile Information: Display name and profile identifiers provided by the third-party services.
  • Content Data: The text prompts and generated visual media that you schedule or publish through our application.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly for the core functionality of the application, which includes:

  • Authenticating your identity with third-party social media APIs.
  • Automating the publishing of carousel posts and text content to your connected social media accounts based on your schedules.
  • Maintaining a history logs of your successful and failed posts for your dashboard.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information or authentication tokens with any third parties. Your data is solely used to communicate with the APIs you explicitly authorized (e.g., sending video posting requests to TikTok).

5. Data Storage and Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect the security of any personal information we process. Authentication tokens are stored securely in our database and are used only for automated posting operations.

6. Data Deletion and Revocation

You have full control over your data. You can disconnect your social media accounts at any time directly from the app dashboard. Upon disconnection, your active authentication tokens and related profile data are immediately deleted from our database. You can also revoke access directly from your social media platform's security settings.

7. Contact Information

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at admin@threadstomation.vercel.app.
