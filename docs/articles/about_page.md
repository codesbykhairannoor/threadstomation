## Welcome to WebAssembly & Dev Tools

**WebAssembly & Dev Tools** is a technical engineering publication dedicated to the next generation of web software: **local-first, sovereign, and zero-cloud architecture**.

We believe modern browsers are powerful operating systems. Instead of forcing users to upload private data to remote cloud servers and pay $20/month SaaS subscriptions, we explore how to compile production-grade native libraries into **WebAssembly (WASM)**, leverage **WebCodecs**, and run compute 100% inside the client's RAM.

---

### The Ecosystem We Build & Document

Our research and articles are paired with real-world, production-grade web applications:

* **[HandleMyFile](https://handlemyfile.com)**: Sovereign in-browser document & PDF suite. Merge, split, redact, and e-sign files with 0 bytes uploaded to remote servers.
* **[SolveMyMedia](https://solvemymedia.com)**: Client-side media processing. Compress 4K video, trim clips, convert formats, and run neural Whisper speech-to-text directly in browser RAM.
* **[HelpMyIMG](https://helpmyimg.com)**: Local photo studio featuring in-browser neural background removal, smart WebP/AVIF compression, and privacy redaction.
* **[CreateMy-QR](https://createmy-qr.com)**: 37 permanent ISO/IEC 18004 QR and barcode tools with high-resolution vector exports and zero expiration traps.
* **[Tranvas](https://tranvas.com)**: The Unified Life OS. Connecting habits, tasks, zero-based budgeting, and WOOP goals in one cohesive dashboard.
