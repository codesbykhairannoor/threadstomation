Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you use Threadstomation, we collect authentication tokens and basic profile information (such as your display name) provided by third-party services like TikTok and Instagram solely for the purpose of publishing content on your behalf.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to authenticate API requests and automate the publishing of carousel posts to your connected social media accounts.

3. Data Storage and Security

Your authentication tokens are stored securely in our database. We do not share, sell, or distribute your data to any third parties.

4. Data Deletion

You can disconnect your social media accounts at any time from the app dashboard, which will instantly delete your active authentication tokens from our database.
